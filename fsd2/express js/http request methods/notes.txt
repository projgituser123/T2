res.send(req.query) // for "flexible routing", and you have to give proper url format, also creates a json object...

=======================================
1. With req.params (Strict Routing)
Yes, you must define the keys in your app.get() path. If the URL doesn't match the structure you defined, Express won't find the route.
Your Code: app.get("/user/:name/:id", ...)
The URL: Must look like /user/pedri/8
The Rule: If you try to visit /user/pedri (missing the ID), you will get a 404 Not Found because Express is looking for that second "segment."
2. With req.query (Flexible Routing)
You use a simple, static route. You do not mention the keys in app.get(). Instead, you just provide the "base" path, and the URL handles the rest.
Your Code: app.get("/search", ...)
The URL: http://localhost:8000/search?name=pedri&id=8
The Rule: The ?name=pedri&id=8 part is not part of the route matching. Express matches the /search part first, then looks at the "extra" stuff after the ? and puts it into req.query.
